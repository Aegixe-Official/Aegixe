# Documentation corrections / 文档纠错

For broken links, unclear instructions or factual corrections, provide the document, section, observation date and a public source where available. Keep public issues focused on documentation.

反馈链接失效、步骤不清或事实错误时，请提供文件、章节、观察日期和可公开核验的来源。公开 issue 仅用于文档讨论。

Do not post credentials, recovery phrases, client materials, private payment records or non-public vulnerabilities. For a sensitive security matter, first use the [website contact entry](https://aegixe.com/contact-us-form/) to ask for an appropriate private receiving channel; do not attach secrets or exploit details to an unconfirmed form.

请勿公开提交凭据、助记词、客户材料、私人支付记录或未公开漏洞。敏感安全问题请先通过[官网联系入口](https://aegixe.com/contact-us-form/)确认合适的私密接收渠道；未经确认的表单不附密钥或漏洞利用细节。

Public availability does not grant a blanket reuse licence. Brand images and documents retain their applicable rights; contact AEGIXE to confirm reuse permission where needed.

公开可读不等于统一授予开源许可。品牌图像与文档适用其各自权利；需要复用时请联系 AEGIXE 确认授权范围。
